Research Area

Doctoral Research Framework
An Intelligent Decision Support System for Early Detection of Ganoderma Disease in Areca Cultivation
Research Problem

Early detection of Ganoderma root disease in arecanut palm cultivation remains severely difficult because pathogen colonisation begins internally in the underground root system and basal stem before visible foliage discolouration emerges. By the time canopy symptoms appear, vascular decay is irreversible.

Research Gap

Existing agronomic studies focus heavily on post-symptomatic visual inspection or laboratory PCR tests. There is limited integration of real-time environmental IoT data with multimodal vision transformers and temporal recurrent networks for sub-clinical early diagnosis.

Research Objectives
  • Preprocess areca image data and environmental IoT sensor telemetry to improve signal-to-noise ratio and guarantee dependable extraction.
  • Extract discriminative disease features across multimodal visual and temporal sensor streams to detect *Ganoderma* patterns.
  • Construct and evaluate an intelligent deep learning framework enabling pre-symptomatic early detection and agronomic decision support.
Multimodal Data Sources
  • Visual Data: Full-tree portraits, basal stem surfaces, crown/canopy density, and visible root-zone characteristics.
  • Environmental IoT Telemetry: Soil moisture, soil temperature, air temperature, atmospheric humidity, and soil pH levels.
